# North American GeoGebra Journal

The **North American GeoGebra Journal (NAGJ)** is a peer-reviewed,
open-access academic publication dedicated to the innovative use of
GeoGebra in mathematics education.

The journal publishes articles that explore the integration of GeoGebra
in teaching, learning, curriculum development, and research across
grades K–16 (kindergarten through undergraduate education).

## Mission

The mission of NAGJ is to provide educators and researchers with a
venue for sharing high-quality work involving dynamic mathematics,
technology-enhanced learning, and innovative approaches to mathematics
education.

## Scope

Topics of interest include:

- GeoGebra applications in mathematics classrooms
- Dynamic geometry and visualization
- Technology-enhanced mathematics instruction
- Teacher education and professional development
- Curriculum design and assessment
- Research on technology-supported learning

## Publication Information

- **Publication model:** Open access
- **Review process:** Open peer review, from 1 September 2026
  (double-blind before that date)
- **Submission fees:** None
- **Publication fees:** None
- **ISSN:** 2162-3856

## Peer review

From its founding in 2012 until 2026, NAGJ operated as a **double-blind**
peer-reviewed journal: the identities of authors and reviewers were concealed
from one another throughout the review process.

**From 1 September 2026, NAGJ is switching to open peer review.** Reviews are
conducted openly on GitHub. Reviewers are named and credited for their work,
the review conversation is public and permanent, and each published article
links to its own review record.

The change accompanies the journal's move from Miami University to the
University of Southern Maine. Refereeing is scholarly labor, and we think it
should be visible and creditable; open review also lets a reader see how a
paper's argument was tested, rather than only that it passed.

Articles published before this date were reviewed under the double-blind model,
and no review records exist for them.

## Reviewers and editorial board

We are actively recruiting **reviewers** and **editorial board members** across
dynamic geometry, linear algebra, statistics education, teacher preparation, and
computational tools for mathematics teaching. If you would like to take part,
[contact the editor](contact.html) with your areas of interest and the software
you work with.

The journal continues the tradition of the original GeoGebra journal
collection and is transitioning to a modern GitHub-based publishing
workflow.
